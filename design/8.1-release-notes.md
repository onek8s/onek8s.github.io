# 8.1 Release notes

**Requirements:** none - the version history of the design.

The design is versioned, one minor version per pull request that changes it and a major version only when a human
says so, newest first; every version is tagged `v<version>` in every repository. Only the current major version is
listed here: the older versions are in the Git tags, the last before v4.0 being
[`v3.8`](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/tree/v3.8). The version is shown in the site's
header and on the covers of the [PDFs](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#the-pdf-and-the-release-notes)
([how to add an entry](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#the-pdf-and-the-release-notes)).

## v4.7 - 2026-10-27

- Every picture of the design has a download for Draw.io under it, the sequence diagram of 3.5 too, whose notes no longer run out of their boxes ([#124](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/124)).

## v4.6 - 2026-10-27

- Every picture is drawn with SVG text in an Arial-sized font, so no label is cut off in a reader's browser or the PDF; pictures 1.1 and 2.3 are laid out again so every line reaches its box, and each has a download for Draw.io under it ([#123](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/123)).

## v4.5 - 2026-10-21

- Page 1.5 Developer experience tells the three journeys of an application team as use cases - onboarding a new application, migrating an existing one and operating the ones in production ([#119](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/119)).
- Requirements R33 (the non-negotiable rules: the patch timetable binds every application, automated tests are the only gate) and R34 (previews per pull request and sandboxes per team inside dev), with their acceptance criteria, R23 and R24 allow jobs, scheduled jobs and queue consumers, and R22 says that an application is reached from outside its namespace only as HTTPS on 443 through the gateway and uses any protocol inside it; the design states platform-owned base images, the non-serving workloads of 3.4 rebuilt and promoted by the base image agent, and the preview and sandbox namespaces of dev ([#119](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/119)).

## v4.0 - 2026-10-20

- Requirements-driven collaboration: the requirements and acceptance criteria are in `requirements/`, owned by humans
  (part 0, page 0.1 How we work), and the design is in `design/`, owned by AI; a required Approvals check asks a human
  to approve a change to `requirements/` and the AI to review a change to `design/`
  ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- The requirement texts of 1.2 are page 0.2 and design 1.2 shows how the design meets each of them; the acceptance
  tests of 7.1 are page 0.3 ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- What no requirement names moved to the unpublished `archive/`: the pages 7.1 Implementation project and acceptance
  testing, 7.4 Outlook and the options kept open and 7.5 Other clouds, sovereignty and exit (its data services
  joined 1.3), and sections of 1.0, 1.2, 2.3 (GPUs), 4.1, 5.1, 5.2, 6.2, 7.2 and 7.3
  ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- Every design page names the requirements it serves under its title, and `check.sh` checks it; every link to a
  requirement jumps to its row in 0.2 ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- The pages with a picture no longer repeat it in prose; the text keeps what the picture cannot show (reasons,
  decisions, numbers, exceptions) ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- Two PDFs: the design with every design page, the summary first, and the requirements
  ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
- The decision log (7.2) and the open questions (7.3) left the design for `archive/`: the design states only what is
  chosen, without decision, sign-off or verification numbers ([#109](https://github.com/onek8s/onek8s.github.io/pull/109)).
