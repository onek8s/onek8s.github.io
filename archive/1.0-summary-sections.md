## What is meant to hold (from 1.0 Summary)

The bets the design spends its complexity on and nowhere else ([section 7.4](7.4-outlook-2030-and-the-options-kept-open.md#what-is-meant-to-hold)):

1. **The application contract is open standards only** – an application written to it in 2026 deploys unchanged in 2030.
2. **Clusters are cattle, cells are the unit of change** – every cluster-level change is a rebuild behind the router, not a migration.
3. **Isolation zones are the blast-radius unit inside a cell**, and can become their own cells later.
4. **Git is the only write path**, for humans and agents alike.
5. **Data lives in managed services**; the stateful cluster is the last option and does not exist until needed.
6. **Private by default**: no public endpoint but the external zones' gateways and the firewall's own address.
7. **The platform operates itself through agents**, inside guardrails humans own.

A feature is added only when something names it, the cloud manages it, it is a per-cell switch, applications do not
notice, and the way out is written before the way in ([D64](../design/7.2-decisions.md#decision-matrix)); everything left out
today is in the [options kept open](7.4-outlook-2030-and-the-options-kept-open.md#the-options-kept-open) with its
trigger.
