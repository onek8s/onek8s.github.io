# 0.1 How we work: requirements-driven collaboration

![How we work](../images/0.1-how-we-work.svg)

The human side is [0.2 Requirements](0.2-requirements.md) and [0.3 Acceptance criteria](0.3-acceptance-criteria.md);
the AI's review checks a human's design change against them, and the [decision log](../design/7.2-decisions.md) records
why the design is as it is.

`CODEOWNERS` and the required check **Approvals** enforce it: the author counts as their own side's approval, and an
approval is kept across later pushes that do not touch that owner's files.

The two folders are two PDFs: the [requirements](https://onek8s.github.io/onek8s-requirements.pdf) and the
[design](https://onek8s.github.io/onek8s-design.pdf).
